Learn by watching it happen

Textbook diagrams freeze a protocol at a single moment. Real protocols are about timing: packets travel, get lost, arrive out of order, and trigger timeouts. Simulations lets you slow that down, pause it, break it on purpose, and read a plain-English log of every decision each side makes.

Every topic pairs a hands-on simulation with a short written explanation. You can run the demo first and read afterwards, or read first and test your understanding by dropping a packet or blocking a path and predicting what happens next.
