About Simulations

Simulations is a free education site that explains networking protocols and computer science concepts step by step, with interactive demos you can poke, pause and break.

Our mission

Many protocols are taught as static diagrams. We believe understanding comes from watching behaviour over time, so every topic includes a simulation with a live event log that explains what each side is doing and why.

What the site covers

We start with reliable data transfer: Go-Back-N and Selective Repeat. Over time we will add the OSI model, the TCP handshake, congestion control and more.

How content is made

All explanations and simulation code are written originally for this site. Simulations run entirely in your browser with no data sent anywhere. Content is simplified for learning, so check official specifications for production work.
