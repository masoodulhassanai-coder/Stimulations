    How to use this simulation

    1. Press Select Next once to step through one scan/swap event, or press Resume to animate continuously.
    2. Watch how the current minimum candidate is tracked and swapped into index i at the end of each scan.
    3. Use Step, Slower (down to 0.25x), Faster (up to 4x), and Reset to run with new values.

    1. Scanning for the minimum

    Starting at position i, Selection Sort sets minIdx = i and scans remaining indices j = i + 1 .. n - 1 to find the smallest value.

    2. Swapping into position

    Once the scan finishes, it swaps arr[i] with arr[minIdx] and advances i, locking another sorted element at the front.

    3. Time & space complexity

    Selection Sort always performs O(n²) comparisons regardless of input order, uses O(1) extra memory, and performs at most n - 1 swaps.
