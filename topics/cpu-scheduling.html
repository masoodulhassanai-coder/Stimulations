    How to use this simulation

    1. Pick FCFS, SJF, SRTF, or Round Robin (and quantum q), then press Dispatch or Resume to watch processes move into the Ready Queue and onto the CPU Core.
    2. Click any active process token to select it, then press Terminate to stop that process.
    3. Use Step to advance one event, Slower (down to 0.25x) and Faster (up to 4x) for animation speed, and Reset to restart.

    1. Ready Queue to CPU Core

    Processes enter the Ready Queue as their arrival time is reached. The scheduler picks the next process according to the active policy—arrival order in FCFS, shortest burst in SJF, shortest remaining time in SRTF, or rotating slices in Round Robin.

    2. Preemption & Time Quantum

    In SRTF, a newly arrived job with less remaining time preempts the current process. In Round Robin, a process runs until it finishes or uses up its quantum q, then returns to the ready queue.

    3. Waiting Time (WT) & Turnaround Time (TAT)

    Each process token displays its remaining burst RT and, once finished, its waiting time WT and turnaround time TAT.
